Why are we looking at this?

Pwllheli has a strong business community and an important role as a commercial and service centre for the Llŷn Peninsula. But businesses across the town face many of the same challenges and opportunities.

A Business Improvement District could provide a way for businesses to work together, identify shared priorities and invest collectively in improvements that benefit the local business environment.

But establishing a BID is a significant commitment. It would involve businesses agreeing to a levy and deciding collectively how that money should be spent.

That's why we are starting with a simple question: would businesses in Pwllheli be interested in exploring it?
