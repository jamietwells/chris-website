We are currently assessing local support for establishing a Business Improvement District (BID) in Pwllheli. A BID has been operating effectively in Caernarfon since December 2015.

Everything on this site is simply an idea! A proposal for making our town better. We want the input of everyone who works or shops in Pwllheli before any plan is put to a vote.

Some ideas of what a BID could do

The cleanest streets in Wales Daily cleaning of the pavements and cobbles, jet washing, chewing gum removal and weeding/landscaping.
More Christmas lights & displays To draw in the crowds and boost footfall. Customers flock to Bicester Village to see the snow machine in action and have a winter wonderland shopping experience!
Peak-season extra cleaning & additional litter bin collections Keeping the town spotless and litter-free in the summer.
A town in bloom again Working with volunteers to refresh hanging baskets and planters, to bring colour to every corner.
Local contracts & youth job opportunities All contracts awarded to local firms. Guaranteed paid work experience for any young person who needs it.
Business growth, training and a national profile for the town Hands-on support and targeted campaigns, especially in the "quieter" months of October to March. Promoting Pwllheli as a national conference venue.
