HAVE A BID?

We are currently assessing local support for establishing a Business Improvement District (BID) in Pwllheli. A BID has been operating effectively in Caernarfon since December 2015.

Everything on this site is simply an idea! A proposal for making our town better. We want the input of everyone who works or shops in Pwllheli before a complete plan is put to a ballot.

CASE STUDY: Read more about the successful BID in Caernarfon, HWB Caernarfon
WHAT IS A BID? LEAVE A COMMENT

Some ideas of what a BID could do

Ultra-clean streets & jetwashing Power-washing pavements and targeted gum removal to make Pwllheli gleam.
Spectacular Christmas lights & events Dazzling festive displays and seasonal magic to draw crowds and boost local trade.
Peak-season extra cleaning & rapid bin emptying Keeping our town spotless, fresh, and welcoming all summer long.
Vibrant floral displays & greening Eye-catching hanging baskets and blooms to bring colour to every corner.
Local contracts & youth job opportunities All contracts awarded to local firms, creating paid work experience for Pwllheli’s young people.
Business growth & off-season boost Hands-on support, masterclasses, and targeted campaigns to power trade from October to March.
