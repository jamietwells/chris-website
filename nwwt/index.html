North Wales Wildlife Trust

Corporate Partnerships Manager – Job Notes

Organisation: North Wales Wildlife Trust

Salary: £30,000 per annum

Location: Hybrid working – office facilities in Bangor

Reports to: Director of Income, Membership & Engagement

BUS 17/17B BUS 18

1. Important dates

  • Application deadline: Monday 7 December 2026, 12 noon.
  • Interview date: Tuesday 15 December 2026.

2. Purpose of the role

  • Grow the Trust's corporate partnerships programme.
  • Build relationships with businesses that want to support nature recovery across North Wales.
  • Generate income through fundraising, sponsorship, donations and corporate membership.
  • Develop long-term relationships with corporate supporters.

3. Main responsibilities

Business development and fundraising

  • Identify and approach potential business partners.
  • Secure financial support, sponsorship and donations.
  • Prepare proposals and presentations.
  • Meet annual income targets.

Relationship management

  • Look after existing corporate supporters.
  • Maintain good relationships.
  • Show businesses the impact of their contributions.
  • Encourage increased support.

Corporate engagement

  • Develop corporate membership.
  • Organise employee fundraising opportunities.
  • Encourage corporate volunteering and conservation activities.

Administration

  • Maintain CRM records.
  • Monitor fundraising targets and income.
  • Prepare reports and support budget planning.
  • Follow GDPR and fundraising regulations.

Networking

  • Attend business events.
  • Meet potential partners.
  • Work with fundraising, communications and conservation teams.

4. What experience and skills are they looking for?

Essential

  • Business development, account management, fundraising or corporate partnerships experience.
  • Relationship-building skills.
  • Experience working towards financial or performance targets.
  • CRM system experience.
  • Writing proposals, presentations or business cases.
  • Excellent communication and organisational skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities.

Desirable

  • Corporate fundraising or sponsorship experience.
  • Employee engagement or volunteering experience.
  • Understanding of ESG and sustainability.
  • Knowledge of the North Wales business community.
  • Welsh language skills.

6. Key things to remember about the role

  • It's primarily a relationship-building and business development job.
  • A big focus is generating income for the Wildlife Trust.
  • You will need to approach new businesses and secure their financial support.
  • You will manage existing corporate relationships.
  • You will have annual fundraising and performance targets.
  • You don't need to be doing practical conservation work; the role is 100% clerical.

7. Questions worth asking at interview

  1. What are the annual fundraising targets?
  2. How many existing corporate partnerships are there?
  3. How much of the role involves finding new businesses versus managing existing relationships?
  4. Is there an existing database of potential corporate partners?
  5. What would success look like in the first six months?
  6. How often would I need to travel around North Wales?

The main thing to remember: Your job is to build relationships with businesses, persuade them to support North Wales Wildlife Trust financially, look after those relationships and grow the income they generate.

This is the central purpose of the position.
