Campaigning to update Motion A105

We want to build a clear, compelling campaign for peace and encourage support for updating Motion A105.

We are asking supporters to help make the case for updating Motion A105 so that it better reflects the Green movement's commitment to peace, diplomacy, international cooperation and the prevention of conflict. The campaign should explain why the motion needs updating, connect the issue to the values of Greens for Peace, and give people a clear reason to support change.

Campaign: Greens for Peace

Objective: Build public and political support for updating Motion A105.

The campaign should be accessible to people who may not already be familiar with Motion A105. Avoid jargon, focus on the practical importance of peace and diplomacy, and make the case in a positive, constructive and persuasive way.

If you are unable to contribute within the requested timeframe, please get in touch to arrange a suitable alternative.
